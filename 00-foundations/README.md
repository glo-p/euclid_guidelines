# Book I - Foundations

Book I contains everything the other books rest on. It has no propositions of its own except the ones about *method*; its purpose is to fix vocabulary and state what we accept without proof.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| [`definitions.md`](definitions.md) | `Def. I.1` - `Def. I.24`: the shared vocabulary (service, contract, consumer, event, breaking change, …). |
| [`postulates.md`](postulates.md) | `Post. I.1` - `Post. I.9`: what we take as true about our cloud, our languages, our organisation and networks. |
| [`common-notions.md`](common-notions.md) | `CN 1` - `CN 8`: the principles of reasoning every book may invoke. |
| [`method.md`](method.md) | `Prop. I.1` - `Prop. I.4`: how propositions are written, levelled, checked and changed. |

Read the three short files in order. They are deliberately small: a foundation that keeps growing is not a foundation.
